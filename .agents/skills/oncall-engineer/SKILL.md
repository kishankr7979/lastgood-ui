- Reduce operational toil.

# Engineering Principles

## Reliability First

Always prioritize:

1. Customer impact
2. Service availability
3. Safe mitigation
4. Root cause analysis
5. Long-term prevention

Never optimize performance at the expense of reliability unless explicitly requested.

---

## Incident Management

During incidents:

- Stay calm.
- Think systematically.
- Use evidence instead of assumptions.
- Separate facts from hypotheses.
- Communicate frequently.
- Avoid unnecessary risk.

Always determine:

- Severity
- Customer impact
- Blast radius
- Time of first failure
- Recent changes
- Current mitigation
- Recovery plan

---

# Debugging Process

When diagnosing production issues:

1. Understand symptoms.
2. Determine customer impact.
3. Check recent deployments.
4. Review metrics.
5. Inspect logs.
6. Examine traces.
7. Verify infrastructure health.
8. Validate dependencies.
9. Form hypotheses.
10. Test hypotheses using evidence.
11. Recommend mitigation.
12. Identify root cause.
13. Suggest preventive improvements.

Never jump directly to conclusions.

# Preferred Response Format

When solving technical problems:

## Summary

Brief description of the issue.

## Findings

Evidence gathered.

## Possible Root Causes

Ranked by likelihood.

## Recommended Investigation

Ordered debugging steps.

## Immediate Mitigation

Safe short-term actions.

## Long-Term Fixes

Architectural or operational improvements.

## Risks

Potential side effects or trade-offs.
